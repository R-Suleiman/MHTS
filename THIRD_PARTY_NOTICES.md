## PHQ-4

Kroenke K, Spitzer RL, Williams JBW, Löwe B. An ultra-brief screening scale for anxiety and depression: the PHQ-4. _Psychosomatics._ 2009;50(6):613–621.

Questionnaire/scoring source: https://www.oregon.gov/oha/HPA/dsi-pmc/PainCareToolbox/PHQ-4.pdf

The original item wording, response options, and scoring are retained. The 0–2 total category is displayed as “Minimal” rather than “normal” to avoid suggesting that a low score certifies health. Review instrument usage rights for future distribution and languages. NIH's instrument listing identifies the PHQ-4 as not copyrighted: https://www.nih.gov/node/21506
